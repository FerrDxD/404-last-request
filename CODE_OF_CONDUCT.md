## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of **404: LAST REQUEST**, pledge to make participation in our community a **harassment-free experience for everyone**, regardless of:

- Age, body size, or disability
- Ethnicity, race, or nationality
- Gender identity or expression
- Level of experience or education
- Personal appearance
- Religion or lack thereof
- Sexual identity or orientation
- Socioeconomic status

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an **open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community**.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Examples of Positive Behavior

These behaviors create a healthy community:

- **Being respectful and empathetic** toward other community members
- **Welcoming newcomers** regardless of their experience level
- **Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback**
- **Acknowledging mistakes**, apologizing, and learning from them
- **Focusing on what is best for the community**, not just for yourself
- **Using inclusive language** in all communication
- **Crediting others' work and contributions** appropriately

### ❌ Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

These behaviors will not be tolerated:

- **Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks** — in public or private
- **Discriminatory language or jokes** targeting any individual or group
- **Sharing others' private information** (doxxing) without explicit consent
- **Sexual language, imagery, or unwanted advances** of any kind
- **Sustained disruption** of discussions, issues, or pull requests
- **Dismissive or condescending behavior** toward questions or contributions from less experienced members
- **Intimidation or threats** — direct or implied

---

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to:

- **Remove, edit, or reject** comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct
- **Temporarily or permanently ban** contributors whose behavior is harmful, threatening, or inappropriate

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may face repercussions as determined by other project maintainers.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to **all spaces managed by this project**, including:

- The GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews)
- Any official communication channels (Discord, email lists, etc.)
- Public or private communications when representing the project

It also applies when an individual is **representing the project or community** in public spaces — for example, using an official project email, posting via an official social media account, or acting as a representative at an event.

## Enforcement Process

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when determining consequences:

### 1. 💬 Correction

**Community Impact:** Minor, isolated inappropriate language or unprofessional behavior.

**Consequence:** A private written warning with clarity on the violation and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

---

### 2. ⚠️ Warning

**Community Impact:** A single incident or pattern of behavior that violates community standards.

**Consequence:** A formal warning with specified consequences for continued behavior. A defined period of no interaction with the people involved. Violating these terms may escalate to a temporary or permanent ban.

---

### 3. 🚫 Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private contact with those involved. Violating these terms may result in a permanent ban.

---

### 4. 🔨 Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** A pattern of violations, sustained harassment, or particularly egregious conduct including aggression or disparagement of any individual or group.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from all forms of community interaction.

---

## Appeals

If you believe enforcement action was applied in error or unfairly, you may appeal by:

1. Contacting the project maintainer directly via email
2. Providing a clear, respectful explanation of why you believe the action was unjust

Appeals will be reviewed in good faith. Abusing the appeals process may itself result in enforcement action.
